# Weekly delivery schedule

The source brief assigns seven relative weeks, without a confirmed start date.
The printed assignment date is 7 November 2026; it is not treated as a submission
deadline. No calendar dates or supervisor signatures have been invented.

| Week | Scheduled work | Deliverable | Verification / exit |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Planning; begin requirements | Supplied week 1 PDF | Preserve feasibility, WBS and risks |
| 2 | Finish requirements; start design | Supplied week 2 PDF | Gate 1 is historical; do not invent evidence |
| 3 | Complete design | Supplied week 3 PDF | Gate 2 approved, as confirmed by student |
| 4 | Implementation foundation | Week 4 report; domain, schema, repository, authentication, build | Build; permission, penalty, password and persistence checks |
| 5 | Complete implementation; start V&V; CI and review | Week 5 report; server, console client, automated suites, CI definition | End-to-end marking and moderation, coverage and defect closure |
| 6 | Complete V&V; Gate 3; deployment v1.0 | Week 6 report; test evidence, installation, role manuals, v1.0 package/tag | Automated checks complete; supervisor Gate 3 and usability review pending |
| 7 | CR1 maintenance; completion | Week 7 report; v1.1 package/tag, regression evidence, consolidated report, viva guide | CR1 tests and original suite; human final review and viva |

Critical path: approved SRS -> approved design -> implementation -> V&V -> Gate 3
review -> operational deployment -> maintenance -> final submission. CI and QA
overlap weeks 4-6. Week 7 packaging here is a reproducible coursework release;
operational use at school still requires the documented Gate 3 review.

The weekly files are work packages prepared in schedule order, not a claim that
seven calendar weeks elapsed or that historical daily Git commits existed.
